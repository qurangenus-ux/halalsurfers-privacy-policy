Privacy Policy

App name: Halal Run: Lantern Rush · Package: com.halalrun.app

Developer: QuranGen Labs · Contact: qurangen.us@gmail.com

Last updated: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Halal Run: Lantern Rush (the "App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our game. We are committed to protecting your privacy. Please read this policy carefully.

In short: We do not collect, store, or sell any personal data. Your game progress is saved only on your own device. The App has no ads, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs — it is fully offline, so nothing ever leaves your device. Details below.

1. Information We Collect

Halal Run itself does not collect any personal information. We have no accounts, no servers, and no analytics of our own.

Data Stored on Your Device (Local Only)

  • Game save data — your progress, best scores, lanterns, unlocked runners and upgrades, daily challenge progress, streaks, settings, and statistics are stored locally on your device (via local device storage).
  • Local crash log — if the App ever crashes, a short diagnostic text file (device model, Android version, stack trace) is written to the App's private cache on your device only. It is never sent to us or to any server; you would have to share it manually if you chose to report the issue.
  • This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or any server.
  • You can erase it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the App.

Permissions

The App requests exactly one Android permission: VIBRATE (haptic feedback during gameplay, controllable in Settings). It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage, photos, phone, or any device identifiers.

2. No Advertising & No Trackers

Unlike many free games, Halal Run contains no advertising of any kind and bundles no advertising or analytics SDKs. Specifically:

  • No ads — no banners, interstitials, or rewarded video.
  • No in-app purchases, subscriptions, or storefront of any kind.
  • No advertising ID is read, collected, or shared.
  • No analytics, attribution, crash-reporting, or tracking services.
  • No personal data is sold, rented, or shared with anyone.

3. No Internet Access

The App is fully offline and does not hold the Android internet permission, so it is technically unable to make any network connection — even in the background. There are no servers, no APIs, and no cloud features. You can play it in airplane mode with full functionality.

The only time a network is involved is when you deliberately tap the optional "rate the app" button, which simply opens the app's own Google Play listing on your device — at that point you leave the App and Google's privacy policy applies.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

We care about children's privacy. Halal Run is rated Everyone and is suitable for all ages. Because the App does not collect any personal information from any user — including children under 13 — no behavioral advertising or profiling is possible, and no advertising ID data is used toward children (or anyone else). If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through any channel outside the App (such as email), contact us and we will delete it.

5. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

  • GDPR (Europe): You have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. Contact us below.
  • CCPA (California): You have the right to know what personal information is collected, to delete it, and to opt out of the "sale" of personal information. We do not sell personal data.
  • Because we store no personal data ourselves, these rights are applied in full by simply uninstalling or clearing app data.

6. Data Security

We do not store your data on our servers, so there is no central database to compromise. Game save data remains on your device, protected by your device's own security and the Android per-app sandbox. Since the App makes no network connections at all, there is no transmission path that could be intercepted.

7. Backup & Device Transfer

Only the App's local game-save preferences (not the crash log) may be included in your device platform's own backup/transfer feature, so progress can be restored if you change phones. Such backups are handled entirely by your device/OS vendor under their privacy policy — we never receive or access any backup copy. You can disable backups in system settings or clear the App's data at any time.

8. Third-Party Links

The App contains no third-party content or advertising. The only external destination is the Google Play Store listing opened by the optional rating button; Google's privacy policy governs that service. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of any third-party website or app you open from outside the App.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, the "Last updated" date at the top will change. Material changes will be announced within the App or on our store listing. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
